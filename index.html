Creative DNA & Vision

Your core taste preferences, guidelines, and goals for Yvi's Ice Cream Lab.

Taste & Texture Preferences

  • Low-to-moderate sweetness; let high quality fruit or botanical tea shine.
  • Silky micro-crystal texture; avoid iciness or excessive gum chewiness.
  • Fresh, bright acidity (lime/lemon accent) on heavy fruit sorbets.

Ninja CREAMi Rules

  • Freeze base for full 24 hours at -18°C.
  • Always flatten top before freezing to protect blade.
  • If crumbly after 1st spin, add 10ml liquid and run 1 Re-Spin.
