## Jasmine Lychee Sorbet (v1.0 — Current Master)
- **Status:** Finalized / ⭐ Favorite
- **Ninja CREAMi Preset:** Sorbet
- **Base Ingredients:**
  - Lychee Puree: 250 g
  - Concentrated Jasmine Tea: 150 ml
  - Fine Sugar: 45 g
  - Fresh Lime Juice: 5 ml
  - Xanthan Gum: 0.5 g
- **Spin Method:** Base spin on `Sorbet` + 1 `Re-spin` with 10ml extra tea if chalky.
- **Notes:** Balanced floral flavor with subtle acidity.
